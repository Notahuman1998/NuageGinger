Our Story

Where tradition meets Nordic wellness

Nuage Ginger was born from a simple belief: that one of Asia's most beloved wellness foods deserves a place on the modern Nordic table.

Crystallized ginger — known as mứt gừng in Vietnamese culture — has been cherished for generations as a remedy, a treat, and a symbol of care. We bring this tradition to Finland with premium ingredients, clean production standards, and packaging worthy of a gift.

The name Nuage — French for "cloud" — captures our vision: something light, refined, and drifting effortlessly between cultures.

The Founder

Bridging cultures through wellness

Hi, I am May, the founder of Nuage Ginger.

As a mother, my family's health is my top priority. I have always been passionate about creating healthy foods — previously developing wellness products, like salads featuring fresh Finnish ingredients, which locals quickly came to love.

Living in Finland, I noticed how deeply Nordic people care about their well-being. I asked myself: Is there anything I can make to bridge my heritage with this lifestyle?

That is when I thought of Ginger. Mứt gừng (crystallized ginger) is a traditional Vietnamese snack that lasts a long time. It perfectly suits the cold Finnish winters, naturally heating the body from the inside out while providing incredible health benefits.
